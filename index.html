ABOUT AFTERGLOW

Even in forgetting,
someone stays.

An exploration of memory, forgetting, and companionship. Fragments drift and leave traces. Where two lights meet, a memory can become whole for a moment.

This is an artistic metaphor, not a representation of every experience of dementia. The original memories are fictional. The companion light is simulated locally, not an online visitor.

Explore with your pointer, touch, or arrow keys. Tab reveals the controls. The Controls button also opens them. Space pauses drifting; Esc closes this window. Sound is off by default.
